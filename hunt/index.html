Hunt for the Pride

RISE FROM CUB TO KING

Start as a small cub in the wild savanna, learn to hunt, find water and keep your hunger under control, then grow strong enough to lead a pride.

Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Survive in an open wilderness: manage hunger, thirst and health
  • Hunt many kinds of animals, from rabbits and deer to zebras, boars, wolves and bears
  • Campaign mode with missions at different difficulty levels
  • Boss missions: attack rival dens and defeat each den's alpha lion
  • Build and protect your pride while rival prides defend their territory
  • Touch controls made for phones and tablets
  • Available in English and Arabic
