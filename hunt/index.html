Hunt for the Pride

Rise from a lion cub to the king of the pride.

Hunt for the Pride is a 3D wildlife survival game for Android. You start as a small cub in the wild savanna, learn to hunt, find water and keep your hunger under control, then grow strong enough to lead a pride.

Features

  • Survive in an open wilderness: manage hunger, thirst and health.
  • Hunt many kinds of animals, from rabbits and deer to zebras, boars, wolves and bears.
  • Campaign mode with missions at different difficulty levels, such as hunting set numbers of the same animal.
  • Boss missions: attack rival dens and defeat each den's alpha lion.
  • Build and protect your pride. Rival prides defend their territory.
  • Made for touch controls on phones and tablets.
