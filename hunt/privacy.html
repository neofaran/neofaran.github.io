Privacy Policy

Hunt for the Pride · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how MacSoft Development Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the Android game Hunt for the Pride ("the Game").

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contact list or precise location.

Game progress (settings, language, missions completed and saved games) is stored only on your device. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, this information is removed.

Analytics

The Game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is used and to improve it, for example which screens and missions are played and whether the Game crashes. Firebase may collect an app-instance identifier, general device information (such as device model, operating system version and language), approximate location derived from the IP address, and usage events. We do not use this to identify you personally. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising and third-party services

The Game shows ads (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads) provided through Unity LevelPlay and ad networks such as Google AdMob. These services may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address and general device information to serve and measure ads, personalise ads where permitted, and prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

See Google's privacy policy and Unity's privacy policy. Each ad network processes information under its own policy.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the analytics and advertising providers described above, who process it under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in Android settings, and you can stop all data collection by the Game by uninstalling it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new date.
