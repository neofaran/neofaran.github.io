Privacy Policy

Galactic Dominion · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how MacSoft Development Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the Android game Galactic Dominion ("the Game").

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contact list or precise location.

Game progress (campaign results, settings, unlocked content) is stored only on your device.

Analytics

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played and to fix problems. It records events such as when a mission, Survival run or Free For All match starts and ends (with the result, difficulty and duration), and whether ads were loaded or shown. Firebase may also collect an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, language, app version and approximate region derived from your IP address. See Google's information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising and third-party services

The Game shows ads (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads) through Unity LevelPlay and its ad-network partners, which may include Google AdMob. These services may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address and general device information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the analytics and advertising providers described above, who process it under their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new date.
