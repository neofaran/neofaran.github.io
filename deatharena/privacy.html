Privacy Policy

Death Arena: Rise of the Warlord · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how MacSoft Development Studio handles information in the Android game Death Arena: Rise of the Warlord .

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contact list or precise location.

Game progress (settings, unlocked vehicles, upgrades) is stored only on your device.

Advertising and third-party services

The Game may show ads provided by Google AdMob and ad-mediation partners. These services may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address and general device information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the advertising providers described above, who process it under their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new date.
